# Phase 2 · Content (`diapo-redacteur`)

**[RULE P2.1]** `travail/02_contenu.yaml`: one entry per page, fields `titre`, `corps`, `bas` (the "so what?" line), `nature` (FACT, INFERENCE or OPINION), `notes`, `sources`, `hypotheses`; the `gabarit` field is added in phase 5.

## The text budget

**[MUST P2.2]** **The text budget**, checked by `portes.py`, limits words, not ideas: an idea that does not fit changes form (table, chart, diagram) or moves to the notes, it does not disappear.

**[MUST P2.3]**

| Element | Slide deck | A4 document |
|---|---|---|
| Title | 12 words, 70 characters | 14 words, 70 characters |
| Body | the shape of the page (table, chart, timeline strip), cells of 8 words; bullets as a last resort, 3 of 10 words, on one page in five at most | paragraphs of 4 lines, one table per quantified idea |
| Visible words per page | 90 at most with an oral presentation, 160 at most without, everything counted except the sources line; a table required by the reference may go up to 250; a page never carries two tables, or a table and a callout box, at the same time | about 300 at most (380 for a proposal or a call for tenders), excluding the sources appendix |
| Bottom line | 20 words | one bold sentence per section, 25 words |
| Notes | 120 words | footnotes or appendix |

**[RULE P2.4]** **Page density**: one dominant visual element (chart, diagram or image, about 30% of the height), a single main table at most, one concluding sentence; the rest goes in the appendix or in the notes. The pieces required by the case are outside these ceilings (words, elements per page, number of pieces). **The decision** fits on two pages at most, as a table, outside the word ceiling.

**[RULE P2.5]** **With an oral presentation**, the page carries the essentials and the notes carry the defense. **Without one**, each page can be understood on its own, the source is visible under each page with figures, nothing essential goes in the notes.

## Reasoning rigor: a toolbox, not a plan

**[RULE P2.6]** **How many pieces**: those the criteria reward or the decision requires, 5 at most in Express, 8 in In-depth; why each one, in `01_cadrage.md`. The others go in the notes or the appendix, or disappear.

**[RULE P2.7]** **Under what name**: each piece takes the name and form the field gives it, never the heading of this list; two people do not hand in the same pages under the same titles.

**[MUST P2.8]** **Mandatory everywhere**: the strongest objection, and an executable recommendation.

**[RULE P2.9]** **Proposal or call for tenders**: one more table (method, sample, criterion fed, decision, gate), and the three requirements that follow.

**[RULE P2.10]** **Budget**: each line carries its basis and its unit price. **External facts** (the price of a provider, a panel, a license, a venue) require a dated source (tariff, public price list, scale), otherwise GAP (a NOT FOUND from phase 3) with the quote to obtain. **Exception, if the recipient expects a priced budget** (call for tenders, proposal, quote: NO.5, rank 1): the price without a source does not leave the total, it stays in it as an ASSUMPTION marked "quote pending" (in a French deliverable, « devis en attente »), with its basis, the quote to obtain and the pending share announced next to the total. **The agency's or the person's own rate** (day rate, fees, margin) is their decision, not a market fact: it enters as a displayed ASSUMPTION, with its justification in one line (level of experience, market range read in phase 3), in the register and in the total. Cumulative commitment at each gate. A GAP line enters no total, announced outside gaps; a "quote pending" line enters the total. Range: a ratio of 1.5 between high and low at most, unless justified in writing. Any saving or gap displayed is computed by script from the same table (trap 14). The rate pages read are counted separately (phase 3).

**[RULE P2.11]** **Conviction**: 6 to 8 assumptions in the register, each tied to a judging criterion and to a decision; a justified weight per criterion; no note without its proof; a starting position on the substance, printed with "what would change our mind", about the client's question, never about the method.

**[RULE P2.12]** **Object of the study**, if the deliverable proposes one: blocks of the questionnaire or guide, indicator of each block, attributes tested, population and its screener (non-consumers included if the framing targets them). Collection from people: the applicable data protection law named, checked as an EXTERNAL line.

**[RULE P2.13]** **Prioritize**: Top 3, ranking, "the most critical is...". No inventory.

**[RULE P2.14]** **The stake in one figure**, with its horizon.

**[RULE P2.15]** **Large estimates**: from the top (total, then shares) and from the bottom (units, prices, customers); beyond a factor of 2 between the two, explain or correct.

**[RULE P2.16]** **Three scenarios** (cautious, central, ambitious), 2 or 3 variables that move, the heaviest ones. **The tipping point** of each key variable.

**[RULE P2.17]** **Every figure is compared** (to last year, to a competitor, to the average, to the target, to the status quo). No false precision: round to what the data allows, distinguish percentages from points.

**[RULE P2.18]** **Two alternatives and the status quo**, judged on the same criteria; write in which case another option would win.

**[RULE P2.19]** **The strongest objection**, on a page or in a callout box, with our answer in figures; **the 2 to 4 unknowns**, each with the test that would resolve it.

**[RULE P2.20]** **The risks**, 3 to 5: probability and its reason, impact in figures, warning signal, countermeasure.

**[RULE P2.21]** **The pre-mortem**: the decision has failed, what are the 3 most likely causes.

**[RULE P2.22]** **A scored matrix** follows 6 rules: 1. weights set before scoring; 2. each level of the scale is a numeric threshold; 3. each cell cites its data; 4. formula displayed; 5. sensitivity in 3 lines (equal weights, dominant criterion halved, weight that reverses the ranking); 6. less than 10% gap is a tie, settled on a knockout criterion. Say, in your own words, that the score is an input to the decision.

**[MUST P2.23]** **The recommendation**, in the vocabulary of the field: decision (with the model sentence from the reference if there is one); 3 quantified conditions; cost and gain with their horizon; 3 to 5 actions (owner: a role or `[to complete]`; deadline; deliverable); 3 indicators, with their starting value, their target and their review date; a stop criterion and the fallback plan; the first step at 30 days.

**[RULE P2.24]** **The assumptions register** (`HYP-01`...), in the appendix: value, range, justification, effect if it varies by 20%; it fits on one page if the appendices fall within the page limit.

**[RULE P2.25]** **The chain table**: for each step of the reference, `02_contenu.yaml` notes what it receives from the previous one and what it gives to the next; the fact-checker verifies that the object leaving one step is the one entering the next.

**[MUST P2.26]** **Uncertainty shows on the page**: a visible tag (ASSUMPTION, ESTIMATE or TO VERIFY, in the deliverable language); a fact carries its dated source; a figure not found is written "not found" (in the deliverable language), never replaced by a plausible value.

**[RULE P2.27]** **The person's voice**: the angle and the lived example taken up in their words, the example as TESTIMONY; their opinion is a starting position, confirmed or nuanced by the figures, never defended against them.

## The oral presentation

**[RULE P2.28]** **With an oral presentation**, also deliver `travail/oral.md`: one script per speaker, at 130 words per minute, 10% margin under the allotted time, a one-sentence transition between speakers; the 5 most dangerous questions from the recipient (jury, client, committee), each with its answer and a pointer to a page or an appendix.
