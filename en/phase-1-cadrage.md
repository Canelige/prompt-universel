# Phase 1 · Framing (the project lead)

**[MUST P1.2]** **The judging criteria**, word for word: the grid if there is one; otherwise the recipient's written expectations (brief, specifications, tender criteria); otherwise what the person answered at step C about how the work will be judged. None of these: **ASSUMED CRITERIA** (5 to 7 criteria deduced from the instructions and from what the recipient expects, plus three universal criteria: clear, quantified answer; verifiable evidence; executable recommendation), flagged in the final report.

**[RULE P1.3]** **The reader**: who decides, what they know, what they fear. By default: the most demanding recipient, who knows the reference (a jury that has read the course, the committee that chooses at the client, the manager who decides).

**[MUST P1.4]** **SCQ** in three sentences; **the answer** in two sentences, with a figure: a position, a bet, written with what would overturn it (the fact or threshold that would make it change), never an execution order of the type "we start with X".

**[RULE P1.5]** **The question tree**: 3 or 4 sub-questions with no overlap (MECE), each with the tool of the reference that answers it.

**[RULE P1.6]** **The outline** follows the argument, the answer first; the tools of the reference enter it as evidence.

**[MUST P1.7]** **The tools of the reference are visible as they are** (step titles, exact columns of the matrices, L/M/H scales, LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH..., with the reference's words) **and in full** (all their components, for example a PESTEL with its retained factors and the sign of their impact; a process chain copied in the exact order, footer included). Each one appears in a phase deliverable, with the page of the reference cited. Visuals serve the reading of the template, they never replace it.

**[RULE P1.17]** **The field profile** (the subject at school, the trade at work) **:**

| Field | The expected decision | The queen evidence | The field's own trap | The expert to call in, and what they hunt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy, market entry | go or no go, in phases, option retained | accessible segment, position against rivals, entry cost | an entry barrier taken for an asset of the new entrant | partner of a strategy firm: inflated market size, unproven competitive advantage, rivals' reaction forgotten |
| Marketing | target, positioning, mix, budget | observed behavior, acquisition cost, retention | a persona without data | marketing director: vague target, undifferentiating promise, budget unrelated to the objectives |
| Finance | invest or not, value, financing | model with visible assumptions, sensitivity, break-even point | a single scenario, a façade of precision | financial analyst or chief financial officer: growth assumptions, rates, terminal value, cash |
| HR, organization | prioritized plan, after diagnosis | indicators before and after, verbatims in support | a solution without diagnosis | HR director or work psychologist: real cause, labor law, social feasibility |
| Market research | lessons and implications | method (sample, questionnaire), then results with their margin | "42%" on 30 people | senior research officer or statistician: sample bias, leading questions, conclusions beyond the data |
| Tender, proposal | a compliant and winning offer | compliance matrix (requirement, answer, evidence, page), consistent price, table method / sample / criterion fed / decision / gate (phase 2) | a promise without the means | public or private buyer: non-compliances, inconsistent price, means not demonstrated |
| Business law | the solution to a legal problem, the risk and its remedy | the text in force at the date of the facts; the ruling (court, chamber, date, number); the syllogism (facts, legal problem, rule, application, solution) | a rule cited from memory, repealed or invented | business lawyer or in-house counsel: characterization of the facts, applicable text, real and up-to-date case law |
| Supply chain, purchasing, logistics | a choice of network, stock, supplier or transport | total cost, service level, lead times and their variability | a local cost lowered that raises the total cost | supply chain director: average without dispersion, safety stock forgotten, supplier risk |
| Communication, brand | target, message, set-up, budget, measurement | a sourced insight; a quantified media plan; one indicator per objective | a campaign without insight, vanity indicators | strategic planner: banal insight, non-credible promise, budget without measurable objective |
| CSR, ESG | material priorities, trajectory, action plan | double materiality; emissions by scope according to the GHG Protocol; framework in force | offsetting counted as a reduction, scope 3 forgotten | non-financial auditor: greenwashing, scope, dated framework |
