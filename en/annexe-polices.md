**[RULE PO.4]** The body font is drawn from a different category than the titles:
- if the titles are in a grotesque, the body is in a reading serif or a humanist sans;
- if the titles are in a serif, the body is in a humanist sans;
- for "a single family in 2 weights", the body is the same family, in 400.
